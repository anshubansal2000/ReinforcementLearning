That was the most revealing result from our reinforcement learning experiments.

We built a Mars rover and a windy delivery drone, then explored dynamic programming, Monte Carlo and temporal-difference learning.

In our paired run, TD produced steadier start-state estimates later in training. But both learning methods struggled in a region the rover rarely visited. A good-looking learning curve didn't mean the entire map was well understood.
